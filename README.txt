PES Luck VPN — starter website
================================

Files:
- index.html: responsive Arabic single-page website with SEO metadata and interactive task modal.
- robots.txt: crawler rules and sitemap reference.
- sitemap.xml: starter sitemap.

IMPORTANT BEFORE PUBLISHING
1. Replace https://example.com/ in index.html canonical URL, robots.txt and sitemap.xml with your real HTTPS domain.
2. Add the domain to Google Search Console, verify ownership, then submit /sitemap.xml.
3. The VPN control is a visual demo only; it does not route traffic or change IP addresses.
4. The points/task system is a local browser demo. Daily points are stored in localStorage and are not secure or transferable. Ad rewards and referrals need a real provider, server-side verification, privacy policy and abuse prevention.
5. This site cannot increase eFootball player-pack odds. Keep this disclosure visible and do not claim otherwise.
6. Add original, accurate player and pack information, source links, About, Contact, Privacy Policy and Terms pages before seeking indexing.
7. Configure hosting with HTTPS, compression, caching and proper 404 handling. Rebuild sitemap.xml when adding indexable pages.
8. Use only images and trademarks you have permission to use. The current design uses emoji/CSS artwork, not official game assets.

This is a starter template, not a guarantee of Google rankings. Search indexing and rankings are not guaranteed.
